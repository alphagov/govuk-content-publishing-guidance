If your draft is [classified as ‘OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE’](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/government-security-classifications), you must select an option to ‘Limit access’ under ‘Settings’. You can limit access by organisations or individuals. This means that only publishers from organisations selected for access or named individuals can see the content before it’s published. Content with limited access will not be copied across to [Integration](/accounts-support/manage-accounts-training/production-integration/).
